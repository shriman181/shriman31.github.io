The Product

Four modules. One platform.

Each module is powerful standalone. Together, they form a complete L&D intelligence loop.

Know exactly what skills live inside your organization.

Skill Radar builds a live, dynamic skills graph of your entire workforce — pulling from HRMS, LinkedIn, performance reviews, and a 15-minute AI-powered self-assessment. No spreadsheets. No surveys. Continuously updated.

  • 800+ skill taxonomy across technical, functional, behavioral, and leadership skills
  • Proficiency scoring (0–5) per skill per employee, with confidence indicators
  • Team-level skill heatmaps and org-wide skills fingerprint for CHROs
  • Skill decay alerts when certifications age or skills go unpracticed
  • LLM-powered inference from GitHub, Jira, and project descriptions

  • Team and org-level gap heatmaps — drill from CHRO view to individual

  • 200+ role benchmarks updated quarterly from live market data

A learning path that actually fits inside someone's week.

Pathmaker auto-generates personalized, sequenced learning journeys for each employee — curated from your LMS, external libraries, and AI-generated micro-content. Time-budgeted to their available hours.

  • Dependency-sequenced paths: Python before Pandas, Docker before Kubernetes
  • Content-agnostic: curates from Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, Udemy, or your own LMS
  • AI Micro-Content: converts internal SOPs and docs into 5-min learning modules
  • Adaptive: path recalibrates if learner moves faster or slower than projected
  • Nudge engine: learning reminders via Slack/Teams sent during low-meeting windows

Our Story

Built by someone who's
lived the L&D problem.

The insight was frustratingly simple: every CHRO I spoke to knew their people needed upskilling. Zero of them could tell me exactly which employee needed exactly which skill by exactly when.

Existing tools either offered content libraries (no intelligence) or enterprise platforms priced for Fortune 500 companies that required 18-month implementations. The Indian mid-market — hundreds of growing tech companies with real L&D budgets and real skills gaps — had nothing built for them.

Optimly is the answer. An AI intelligence layer that connects to your existing systems, speaks the language of your HRMS, and delivers personalized learning at the speed that modern organizations need to move.
